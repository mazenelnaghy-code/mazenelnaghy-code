### [Dog detection and humane repeller](https://github.com/mazenelnaghy-code/dog-detection)

**Graduation project · award winner**

A Raspberry Pi camera that spots stray dogs with YOLO11n and drives them away with a humane
ultrasonic repeller, built to keep kindergartens and school gates safe.

- **97%** of dogs found on PC and **96%** on the Pi, up from 69% for the original prototype (236 Egyptian street-dog photos)
- **4%** false alarms on photos of children
- Web dashboard, 100+ tests, one-command demo in Colab, Codespaces or Docker

[Project page](https://mazenelnaghy-code.github.io/#/projects/dog-deterrent) · [Source](https://github.com/mazenelnaghy-code/dog-detection)
